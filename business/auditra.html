Audit workflow

From records to corrective action

  1. 01Collect records

    Books, reports, invoices, statements, schedules, and supporting evidence.

  2. 02Verify & reconcile

    Cross-check entries, balances, documents, approvals, and physical or system evidence.

  3. 03Analyze P&L

    Understand revenue, margin, costs, expenses, variance, and profit movement.

  4. 04Report findings

    Present errors, risks, gaps, exceptions, financial impact, and control observations.

  5. 05Close actions

    Assign corrections, responsible owners, priorities, deadlines, and follow-up checks.
