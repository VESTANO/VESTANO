# Website UI test — 28 October 2026

## Result

The site generally works across desktop, tablet, and mobile, but four issues need attention. This was a testing task; application HTML, CSS, and JavaScript were not changed.

## Findings

1. **Medium — Homepage overflow at 320px.** The document measures 325px wide. The Mission and Vision layout extends beyond the viewport, including its cards and image. Some About card headings also exceed their available text width at this size. Reproduce at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/` with a 320px viewport. Inspect `.mission-vision-band`, `.mission-vision-copy`, and the About headings. Allow grid children to shrink and wrap long words, then repeat the narrow-screen check. Evidence: `finding-index.html-320.png` and `responsive-results-2026-10-28.json`.

2. **Medium — The Market overflow at 320px.** The document measures 339px wide. “Leadership Driving the Retail Transformation” extends beyond the available content width. The founder name also exceeds its text column. Reproduce at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/the-market.html`, width 320px. Make the section heading's grid child shrink and wrap text safely. Evidence: `verified-findings-2026-10-28.json`.

3. **Medium — The Market leadership cards are cramped on phones.** At 320–390px, each portrait remains beside the biography. This leaves a very narrow text column, creates tall cards, and makes names and paragraphs awkward to read. At 320px, “Sudheesh” extends outside its column. Stack the portrait above the copy at phone widths. Relevant rule: `.market-leader-card` and its mobile override in `styles.css`. Evidence: `finding-the-market.html-320.png` and `sheet-visual-the-market.html-390.png`.

4. **Medium — “Choose region” has no action.** The homepage header renders this button, but `script.js` has no handler or region selection flow. Add the intended region selection behavior or remove the control until it is supported. Source: `index.html`, line 123.

## Coverage and passing checks

- 48 current source HTML pages at widths **320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920px**: 432 page/width combinations in Chromium.
- All 432 loads returned HTTP 200; no uncaught page JavaScript errors were recorded. No completed image loads failed in these checks.
- Horizontal page overflow appeared in two combinations: homepage at 320px and The Market at 320px. The remaining 430 combinations had no document-level horizontal overflow.
- Static audit included the source tree and existing `dist` copy: 95 HTML files, 1,069 anchor references, 367 image elements, and 212 static button elements. No missing local files, anchor targets, duplicate IDs, or missing image alt attributes were reported.
- 63 recorded interaction scenarios covered the five homepage navigation menus, the 13-link business dropdown, matching and empty search states, carousel next/previous and end boundaries, assistant open/reply/close, and nine visible project gallery routes. These controls responded as expected at the tested widths.
- Six additional contact modal scenarios at widths 320–1920px and viewport height 667px: dialog remained inside the viewport and closed with Escape.
- Visual review covered homepage, Market, Projects, Sign, business directory, and retail consulting layouts on phone and desktop screenshots. Targeted image decode checks confirmed that temporarily empty About and Mission image panels populate successfully.
- Homepage business-card descriptions deliberately use a two-line ellipsis. This is intentional truncation, rather than accidental clipping.
- The BPOS image deliberately extends into an overflow-hidden image frame. Its copy and CTA remain readable; this automated clipping flag was not counted as a text defect.
- A decorative offscreen element on Fanzzy did not enlarge the page and was not counted as content overflow.

## Empty space and cards

No persistent large empty section was confirmed in the reviewed layouts after scrolling and waiting for image decode. The dark sections are the site's background styling. Images can temporarily leave empty frames while loading during fast scrolling. The confirmed card issue is the narrow biography column on The Market's phone layout.

## Limits

Tests used local Chromium emulation, not physical phones or Safari/Firefox. Width scans used a 900px height; targeted checks also used 800px and 667px heights. Not every width/height combination was tested. Phone/email links, WhatsApp, external map destinations, and microphone permission flows were not activated. External destination uptime and production hosting were not checked. The Sign brochure rendered in the reviewed browser screenshot; every brochure page/control was not exercised.
