# UI fixes and retest — 28 October 2026

All four reported issues have been addressed in the website source.

## Changes

- Homepage Mission and Vision uses its container width without an extra viewport-based inset. Its grid copy can shrink, and headings wrap safely.
- Homepage About and Mission headings use smaller fluid type on narrow phones so long words fit without spilling out of cards.
- The Market section grid permits its columns to shrink. Long section headings wrap within their allocated space, and the hero title scales down at the narrowest phone sizes.
- The Market leadership cards stack each portrait above its biography at widths up to 760px. Founder names and body copy use the full card width.
- Removed the unused “Choose region” button from the homepage header. There is no configured region list or region navigation to support it.

Application changes are limited to `index.html` and `styles.css`. Existing unrelated local changes were preserved. No deployment was performed.
